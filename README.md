# My Favorite Series: The Walking Dead

**The Walking Dead** is an *American post-apocalyptic horror drama* based on the comic book series by Robert Kirkman. Spanning **11 seasons** from 2010 to 2022, the story follows a tight-knit group of survivors fighting to stay alive and keep their humanity in a world overrun by flesh-eating *"walkers"*.

---

## Why I Love It
* **Intense Survival Drama:** Every episode balances walker threats with complex conflicts between rival human communities.
* **Unforgettable Character Growth:** Characters evolve dramatically under pressure—from timid survivors to fearless leaders.
* **Iconic Villain Arcs:** Antagonists like *The Governor* and *Negan* raise the stakes and test the group's loyalty.

## Top 3 Favorite Characters
1. **Daryl Dixon** – A fiercely loyal tracker, survivalist, and crossbow expert who always has the group's back.
2. **Glenn Rhee** – The resourceful former pizza delivery guy whose courage, optimism, and quick thinking save the group countless times.
3. **Carol Peletier** – One of the best character arcs on television, transforming from a quiet survivor into a strategic and unstoppable protector.
